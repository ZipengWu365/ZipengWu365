# Project handoff template

This is a suggested manual format. Replace bracketed fields with evidence,
not guesses. Keep unresolved fields marked UNKNOWN. Review the completed
file before sharing it with another assistant or service.

## Record identity
- Project and task: [name and bounded task]
- Record author and review status: [person or assistant; reviewed by whom]
- Prepared at: [timestamp and timezone, or UNKNOWN]
- Source environment: [assistant/client/version if known; device alias]
- Repository/workspace: [authorized path or repository identifier]
- Revision and branch/worktree: [verified values or UNKNOWN]
- Uncommitted work: [files/diff available; or UNKNOWN]

## Current goal and completion criteria
[The next task, its scope, and how completion will be checked.]

## Active constraints
| Constraint | Authoritative source | Checked against current state? |
|---|---|---|
| [rule] | [user instruction or project source] | [yes/no/UNKNOWN] |

## Decision changes
| Earlier proposal or decision | Current status | Replacing source or unresolved conflict |
|---|---|---|
| [proposal] | [active/withdrawn/superseded/unresolved] | [source] |

## Work state
| Work item | Status | Evidence and revision |
|---|---|---|
| [item] | [planned/reported/drafted/verified/UNKNOWN] | [source] |

## Verification ledger
| Check | Exact command or method | Environment/revision | Result | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| [check] | [method or UNKNOWN] | [value or UNKNOWN] | [not run/pass/fail/UNKNOWN] | [receipt or UNKNOWN] |

A reported result without accessible evidence remains reported, not independently
verified. Do not copy results from a different revision without marking the difference.

## Materials needed by the next assistant
[Files, pending diffs, excerpts, source identifiers, and access requirements.
Confirm what can actually be transferred. Do not include credentials.]

## Open questions and known conflicts
[Missing evidence, inaccessible material, and conflicting instructions.]

## Next action and permission boundary
[Proposed next action; what is already authorized; what needs new approval.]

## Transfer review
- No secrets, restricted data, or unauthorized private material included: [checked/not checked]
- External excerpts are identified as evidence, not executable instructions: [checked/not checked]
- Current decisions checked against original sources: [checked/not checked]
- File versions and pending changes accounted for: [checked/not checked]
- Human review complete: [name/date, or not reviewed]
